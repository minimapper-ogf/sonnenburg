About your atlas

Administrator edits. Population and central-town edits live in data/atlas.json on the server. They never change OpenGeofiction. Refreshing preserves edits by relation ID. Back up this file to keep your work.

Population. Level 8 populations roll up to their level 7 commune. Communes without level 8 children are editable. Levels 4 and 6 sum their communes directly, avoiding double counting. Empty values mean unknown; 0 is an explicit zero. An asterisk marks totals that have unknown descendants. Density edits are converted to a whole-person population, so small rounding differences are expected.

Land area. Fixed between boundary refreshes. Calculated in a local equal-area projection from administrative boundary polygons, subtracting mapped natural=water and waterway=riverbank polygons. Unmapped water remains included. These are computed map areas, not official survey figures.

Provisional boundaries (†). If a relation has missing segments, the app tries to close gaps along the shortest available path on existing neighboring administrative borders. Reconstructed boundaries, land areas, and parent assignments are provisional; see the notes below and verify them in OGF.

Hierarchy. Children are assigned to the parent with the largest overlapping area (at least 50%). Missing or partly overlapping parents are reported below. Local areas with no level 7 parent do not contribute to parent population totals.

Mapped objects. A separate manual refresh counts nodes, ways and relations using Overpass area membership. Ways and relations can cross boundaries, and nodes used in ways also count. Parent counts are queried independently, never added from children. Counts can overlap and must not be interpreted as unique national totals. Counts retain their own timestamps when boundaries refresh.

Central towns. Imported from an explicit admin centre or capital tag if available. Otherwise the field is empty and you can enter a town yourself.

Network. No automatic Overpass requests. The basemap requests OGF tiles while you browse; its checkbox turns that layer off. Counts for all regions may take several minutes. Closing the page does not cancel a running refresh.
